# Runner guide

Open [Woodland Woodchuckers](https://woodland-woodchuckers.web.app). You can browse published runs, routes and milestones before joining. Check the run card for the event's date, start time, venue and available routes.

## Join or sign in

1. Choose **Join Club** or **Sign In**.
2. Use Google or request a passwordless email sign-in link. Use the same email each time so attendance stays with one account.
3. On your first visit, complete your runner profile. Check your name and shirt size; staff use these for recognition and gear.
4. If you ran with the club before, claim only historical names that belong to you. You can also do this later.

Open your profile button to find **Edit profile**, **My Runs**, **Claim historical runs**, notifications and **Sign Out**. Staff have an additional Organizer tools section; ordinary runners do not see staff controls.

## Find a run and RSVP

Runs lists upcoming events first. **Show prior runs** opens older events. Select a route to see its map and details.

When signed in, choose **RSVP** to mark yourself as going; choose **Going** to remove your RSVP. RSVP helps planning, but attendance and mileage are recorded by check-in.

Use **Share run** to send the public run link. **Add to Cal** downloads that event to your calendar. The schedule's calendar subscription menu offers an updating club feed; your calendar provider controls refresh timing.

## Check in on run night

1. Open the run's **Check In** button or scan the table QR code.
2. Sign in if prompted, then enter the code word announced at the run.
3. Choose the route you are completing, an allowed loop count, running or walking, and whether you are participating with a pet.
4. Submit and wait for confirmation. The app may then show badges or milestones you earned.

Check-in opens **two hours before the run starts** and closes at **midnight in the club's Pacific time zone**. The table QR link opens an available event; when more than one is open, the code word identifies it. One runner receives one attendance record per run. An RSVP alone does not count.

You can reopen your check-in during the window to edit the available choices. Repeating or editing a check-in does not add a second run. If you need help, ask the table team: organizers can assist registered runners within the same window. Corrections after closing go through an admin.

Check-ins need a confirmed online save. If the app reports a connection or save problem, keep your choices and retry when connected. Viewing cached pages offline does not record attendance.

## View attendance and claim older runs

Open **profile → My Runs** to view your dated attendance, route/mileage details where recorded, and a separate summary of undated historical credits. Use **Next**, **Previous** or **Refresh history** as needed. Pages show 25 dated records at a time; older records are retained.

Open **Claim historical runs** from the profile menu or My Runs. Search for an old sign-in name, select it, review the information and confirm it belongs to you. Repeat for additional names you used. Shared dated runs count once across claimed names; undated credits remain additive. Missing historical dates or mileage are not invented.

If a saved claim was interrupted, use **Finish interrupted claim**. If a name is already claimed by someone else or the records look wrong, ask an admin to review it. Members cannot transfer or unclaim names themselves.

## Routes and maps

Open **Routes**, select a route and use its map, available turn-by-turn directions and elevation profile. **Download GPX** saves the route for a compatible watch or navigation app. Some routes also have Garmin, Strava or RunGo links.

**Show My Location** / **My Location** asks for location permission and shows your position on the map. It does not check you in or automatically record a workout. GPS and map availability depend on your device, permission settings and connection; review the route before setting out.

## Milestones and gear

**Milestones** shows the club's current rewards and badges. **Standings** shows member statistics when signed in. Staff can see pending gear and mark it delivered when you collect it. Keep your shirt size up to date in **Edit profile**. For missing attendance or an incorrect award, ask an admin rather than creating another account or check-in.

## Install, notifications and updates

- iPhone/iPad: open the site in Safari, use **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open the installed app.
- Android: use **Install app** or the browser's install menu.
- Enable notifications for each device using **Enable Alerts** or the profile menu, and allow them when the device asks. On iPhone, use the installed Home Screen app.
- Open the bell for in-app announcements. **Mark all as read** clears the unread indicator.
- For an old or stuck version, select the version button in the footer or profile menu, then **Check for Updates & Refresh App**. Finish or cancel any unsaved form first.

Announcements appear in the app. Phone pushes currently come from Firebase Console; creating a run does not automatically send a push or reminder.

See [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for common problems, or ask a club organizer for help.
