# Troubleshooting and FAQ

Start with the [runner guide](runner-guide.md) or [organizer guide](organizer-guide.md) for the normal workflow.

## I cannot sign in or my history is missing

Use the same Google/email address you originally registered. A different email is a different account. If using an email link, request a fresh link and open it in the intended browser/app; follow any request to confirm the email address.

If an installed app gets stuck during sign-in, open its version button and choose **Check for Updates & Refresh App**, then retry. Check your connection. Ask an admin to review account/history issues before making another account. Share the error wording and app version, not a sign-in link or password.

## Check-in is unavailable

Check the run date and time: the window opens two hours before start and ends at midnight Pacific. You must sign in, complete registration and enter the run's code word. The code comes from the event team, not the public share link. For overlapping events, confirm you are checking into the intended run.

Staff can help registered runners inside the same window. A closed window has no organizer/admin bypass. If the club is paused for maintenance, wait for an admin to resume changes.

## I RSVP'd, but my run or miles did not count

RSVP records your intention to attend. Check-in records attendance. Complete check-in and wait for confirmation. Then look in **My Runs**, using **Refresh history** if necessary. Do not create duplicate attendance to fix totals; ask an admin to review a confirmed save that is missing.

## My route, loops or activity are wrong

Reopen your check-in during the window and edit the available choices. Mileage follows the saved route and permitted loop count. Table Mode assistance does not overwrite an existing check-in. For a closed event or a historical record, ask an admin to review the correction.

## My old run count differs from the sum of my claimed names

Shared dated attendance counts once across claimed names. Undated historical credits remain additive and appear separately in My Runs; they do not receive made-up dates or mileage. Select **Finish interrupted claim** if offered. Names already claimed by another account require admin review; creating another account will not transfer them.

## My Runs shows only part of my history

Dated records appear in pages of 25. Use **Next** / **Previous** and check the separate undated-credit summary. Missing details in old imports are labeled as unavailable. A missing/deleted recorded run or route can make its link unavailable without deleting the attendance credit.

## The map is blank or cannot show my position

Check your connection and retry. Map tiles and street data come from external services and may be temporarily unavailable. Location needs permission in both browser/app and device settings. Try **Show My Location** or **My Location** again after allowing it. GPS can be inaccurate or unavailable indoors.

A location dot does not record a workout or check you in. Download the GPX in advance for a compatible navigation app when useful. Downloading a GPX does not guarantee the web app's full map will be available offline.

## I enabled alerts but received no phone notification

Enable notifications on the device you are using; registration is per device. Check browser/app permission and device notification settings. On iPhone, open the installed Home Screen app before enabling alerts.

The bell's in-app announcements and phone pushes are separate. Admins currently send phone pushes from Firebase Console. Publishing a run or announcement does not automatically send a phone notification, and automatic reminders are not implemented. Delivery still depends on the device and network.

## The app looks old or a button is missing

Open the version button in the footer or profile menu and choose **Check for Updates & Refresh App** after finishing unsaved work. Reopen the app and confirm you are signed into the intended account.

Staff actions appear in **Organizer tools**; ordinary runners do not receive them. Admin Portal is admin-only. If your assigned role looks wrong, ask an admin to review it. An icon retained by the phone can remain old even after the website updates.

## Can I use the app offline?

Previously loaded content may be available offline. Sign-in, claims, RSVP, check-in and staff changes need a confirmed online save. A pending or failed save is not a completed action. Reconnect, retry and wait for confirmation.

## Why does social posting say it is unavailable?

Facebook/Instagram account access and server activation are still pending. You can save the run independently and use **Share run** to share its public link. Route-photo social publishing will become available after configuration and acceptance; no post is sent merely by saving a run or opening the preview.

## What should I send when asking for help?

Tell the club team the run title/date, what you tried, the visible error, your device/browser and the app version. For account issues, give your registration email privately to an authorized admin. Avoid sharing code words publicly, email sign-in links, access tokens or another runner's profile/emergency contact information.
